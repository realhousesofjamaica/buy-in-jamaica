    NHT's published rules say the first payment is due when you register, and a new contributor's waiting time is 2 years from registration (1 year for a previous contributor, who may pay that year as a lump sum but still has to wait it out). So a backdated start can bring forward the date you reach 104 weeks, but you still can't apply before the waiting time from registration ends. Get NHT to confirm in writing that they'll accept a backdated start date for you.

    Find the right lender

    Only NHT's External Financing Mortgage Programme (EFMP) partners can process your NHT loan and a top-up together. From the US, start with the lenders that have US offices or US toll-free lines. Then check that they handle your loan type (some partners haven't offered construction loans) and accept US income from an overseas voluntary contributor.

    Questions to ask every lender

    • Do you process NHT EFMP loans for overseas voluntary contributors with US income?
    • Do you offer my loan type under EFMP (build on own land, open market, house lot)?
    • How much will you finance in total for a non-resident, and what deposit do you need?
    • What's your rate on the top-up or mortgage, is it fixed or variable, and for how many years?
    • What do I pay upfront (application, valuation, legal, stamp duty), and do you charge processing fees before approval?
    • Can I sign and close from the US, or do I need a Power of Attorney in Jamaica?
    • How long from full documents to disbursement?

    If a partner lender turns you down, NHT says it will process the NHT loan you're entitled to through its own branches.
